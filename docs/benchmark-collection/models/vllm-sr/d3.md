### OSWorld (osworld-v2.1)

### Chartography (chartography)

- 结论：不适用。

已检查的主要资料：
- [vLLM-SR D3 official model card](https://huggingface.co/vllm-sr/d3)
- [Chartography protocol paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10677v1)

研究备注：
- Chartography requires chart-image interpretation followed by free-form text answers. D3 can accept images but only returns restricted structured decisions, so it cannot follow the fixed answer protocol.

### Humanity's Last Exam (humanitys-last-exam)

- 结论：不适用。

- [Humanity’s Last Exam official site](https://lastexam.ai/)

研究备注：
- The original HLE evaluates final academic answers across the fixed 2,500-question set. D3 can choose among supplied options or answer yes/no/score, but cannot produce the arbitrary short or open-ended answers required by the full protocol.
