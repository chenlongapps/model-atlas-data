### DeepSWE (deepswe-v1.1)

- 结论：不适用。

已检查的主要资料：
- [Deepgram Flux TTS official release article](https://deepgram.com/blog/text-to-speech-comes-of-age-deepgram-launches-conversation-native-speech)
